Service terms

Pre-release notice · last updated July 20, 2026. This operational draft is not a substitute for counsel and must be approved for the production entity and jurisdiction before accepting customers.

Service model

Plans include a platform-credit allowance. Templates consume a published fixed credit price; custom agents consume a base price plus selected ingredient prices. Credits cover Agent Oven orchestration and do not include third-party AI inference. Customers provide and control supported provider credentials.

Agent responsibility

Customers configure agent purpose, knowledge, authority, budgets, and approvals. High-impact actions remain subject to configured human and policy gates. Customers must not use the service unlawfully or supply credentials in content fields.

Availability and changes

Pre-release features may change and are provided without a production SLA until a signed order states otherwise. Agent definitions depend on the hosted control plane for policy, credit admission, evidence, and execution leases.
