# Open VSX: free extension, optional services from December 15, 2026

FactoryLine's Open VSX extension and local proof core remain free. Open VSX is
the distribution channel, not the billing or entitlement system. This follows
the Eclipse Foundation's [Open VSX Registry FAQ](https://www.eclipse.org/legal/open-vsx-registry-faq/),
which states that registry extensions are made available without fee; optional
services are therefore sold separately rather than by restricting the download.

Every capability shipped before the commercial transition is free through
**December 14, 2026 at 11:59:59 PM Eastern time**. Beginning **December 15,
2026**, Code Factory may offer two optional services outside the registry:

- **Proof Pro — $9 USD/month or $90 USD/year:** advanced proof, hosted
  proof-aware continuity, authorized cross-workspace evidence recall, and
  retention/export controls. Early adopters may retain the grandfathered
  $5.95/month or $60/year Founding Proof Pro price.
- **AppForge Builder — $24 USD/month or $240 USD/year:** Proof Pro plus
  AppForge storyboards and design contracts, SaaS promise-to-permission proof,
  and supervised Junie-compatible missions. Hosted model, build, render,
  device, and provider usage is metered separately.
- **Team Assurance — $20 USD per active contributor/month with an annual
  commitment, or $24 monthly (three-contributor minimum):** shared proof
  history, policy and approval routing, governed exceptions, and evidence
  exports.

These prices do not apply to downloading the extension, running local IDE
commands, the local CLI or MCP server, local Studio or Graph Ops, or local
receipts and verification gates. Those remain available without a commercial
account.

## Activation boundary

The schedule is approved; paid activation is not live yet. Before December 15,
the owner must attach dated evidence for terms/privacy/refunds, checkout and tax
disclosure, entitlement and offline-failure behavior, retention/export/deletion,
support and incident ownership, and final product-owner activation. Until all
six gates pass, no checkout, payment, entitlement, or paid-service availability
may be claimed.

The machine-readable source of truth is
[`OPEN_VSX_MONETIZATION_2026.json`](OPEN_VSX_MONETIZATION_2026.json). It keeps
commercial activation human-controlled and explicitly prevents automation from
charging a user or restricting the free registry artifact.
